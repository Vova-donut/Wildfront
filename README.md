# Wildfront

A competitive real-time PvP strategy game where cute animals become fearless warriors.

Build your deck, deploy units across the battlefield, and outplay your opponent using strategy, positioning, and unique unit abilities.

> ⚠️ Wildfront is currently in active development.

---

# Gameplay

Wildfront is a multiplayer strategy game inspired by real-time deck builders while introducing its own gameplay mechanics.

Players build a deck before the match and use energy to deploy units onto the battlefield. Every unit has unique abilities, strengths, and weaknesses, allowing for different strategies and playstyles.

The goal is simple:

Destroy your opponent's base before they destroy yours.

---

# Features

- 🎮 Real-time PvP battles
- 🐾 Unique animal units
- 🃏 Deck building
- ⚡ Energy management
- 🧠 Strategy-focused gameplay
- 🌐 Online multiplayer
